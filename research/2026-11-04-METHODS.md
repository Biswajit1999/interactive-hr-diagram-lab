# Gaia parallax sensitivity (conditional numerical study)

Run from the research directory: `python -m unittest -v test_gaia_uncertainty.py`.

Three independent diagnostics: (1) parallax-to-absolute-G transformation, (2) first-order G/parallax covariance propagation, (3) sensitivity to assumed additive parallax offsets. For parallax in mas, M_G = G + 5 log10(parallax_mas) - 10; dM/dparallax = 5/(ln(10) parallax). No catalogue rows are modified or analyzed. The inverse-parallax estimator is only suitable for high signal-to-noise parallaxes and ignores extinction, selection, Gaia zero-point spatial/color/magnitude dependencies and nonlinear low-SNR posterior effects. This is a synthetic/analytic validation, not a new Gaia inference.
